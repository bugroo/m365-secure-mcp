# Community adoption gates

No package, container, Registry entry or public launch is authorized by this
document. It records the review gates for the future Community Adoption
Program under the [project north star](PROJECT_NORTH_STAR.md).

Before the first public launch:

- all three Operational Playbooks v1 execute through signed fixed contracts;
- a clean-machine quickstart and safe synthetic demo are reproducible;
- recorded playback and reviewed non-production live-lab tests pass;
- the public Evaluation Suite reports configuration-qualified results;
- the MCP client compatibility matrix is verified;
- release provenance, artifact attestations and SBOM binding are produced by
  the protected release workflow;
- community health files and the new-operation contributor scaffold are
  complete;
- `server.json` is reviewed before future publication;
- MCP Registry publication is reviewed while the Registry remains preview;
- an OpenSSF Baseline/Best Practices assessment is recorded;
- an external reviewer approves all T3 operation semantics.

External credentials, customer policies, tenant inventory, WERIXO integrations
and commercial operational data remain outside this Apache-2.0 repository.
